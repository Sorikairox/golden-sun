## 3. Checked, no port bug found

0 distinct stubs hit on every route. Compared with the vendor sources (behaviour and timing): the pond's jump cells appear only
after the stump falls (MapGrid_CopyCellAttributes(11, 36, 5, 5, 11, 6)); the sunk east pillar's cells when entering from the
east side; the west pillar's two places and their cell copies; Move's error/no-target cases; the Mind Read wave and tint;
the Djinni battle and join tint; the Kolima curse scene's fades (SOUND_FADE_OUT_BGM) and its domes flickering on even/odd frames.

- **Interlaced wipe between cave rooms** (WINDOW_TRANSITION_INTERLACED_WIPE, e.g. f11686-11710 of goma_forge): the part of the
  screen a window has covered looks grey in thumbnails. Pixel check: even lines inside WIN0 are black (WININ 1 = BG0 only, BG0's
  map all tile 0 = transparent) and odd lines show the old scene in colour (24/20/7 rock) until WIN1's opposite wipe reaches
  them; the HBlank DMA writes WIN0H/WIN1H as one word per line (DMA0 ctl a660). Matches ScreenTransition_UpdateScanlineTable.
  Not a bug.
- **Kolima's silent windows** (audio, section 5): the curse scene fades the music out three times (kolima.c 831, 1001, 1519) and
  plays sound effects only; silence between them is the game's.
- **Mind Read's tint cycling** (blue, purple, brown, green within one message): the hue shifts with the frame counter
  (mind_read.c). Not a bug.
- A battle lost to Forge sends the party to the last Sanctum (Vault) with full HP, as the game does.

## 4. Tools (committed)

- `tests/probes/hpath.cjs` (test-only): a height- and layer-aware path search from the leader's cell at HPATH_AT. Ground
  heights come from the shape's terrain kind and samples (the 14 kinds of gMapGrid_TerrainHeightFunctions, terrain.c,
  re-modelled in JS; the two 256-byte tables read from the ROM at run time); a step is legal while the ground ahead is at most
  8 px higher and 12 px lower (MapGrid_CheckCollision), behavior 255 blocks; neighbour walks are traced pixel by pixel; jump
  cells (254/249) jump two cells when the middle is not too high (a wall in the middle is jumped), climb cells (253) join their
  column, ledges (252) drop south; HPATH_JUMPW=20 adds Goma Cave's west gap jumps. Layers: gMapGridWork's layers[] are 0x30
  apart and are windows into the 128-wide grid (the pillar room's layer 2 is layer 0's rows 50 columns east); a cell's picture
  bits 13-12 move an actor to that layer (actor_camera.c), and the search follows them. Prints the path as runs and as autowalk
  waypoints (HPATH_TO), a reachability map (HPATH_MAP=1) or every cell's centre height (HPATH_MAP=2).
  It found every cave route this session at the first try except where actors stand (HPATH_BLOCK) or Move/scene events change
  cells (search again after them).
- `tests/probes/mapdump.cjs` MAP_MODE=height: prints shape byte 1 itself (8-px units, signed: 4 = y 32, '-' below 0); it
  divided by 16 before, so y 32 printed 0.

Scratch helpers (not committed): port_work/g18/{e.sh, walk.sh, run.sh, act4.cjs, io.cjs (display registers), bg0*.cjs, dma.cjs,
lay.cjs, audio.sh, k*.txt/m_k*.txt/n_k*.txt/b_k*.txt/f_k*.txt (key legs), *chain.txt}.

## 6. Routing notes

- Move's range is 32 px (Field_GetPsynergyTargetRange: kind 1), counted 2/3 for ACTOR_NEAR_PSYNERGY targets (the stump). Across
  a gap the leader stops a few pixels short of the edge: hold the direction into the edge first (~30 frames), then cast.
- Examine/talk reaches further than Mind Read's 32 px in front (the Bilibin tree man answered A from 30 px and from a 26 degree
  diagonal), so A there examines instead of opening the menu: set Mind Read as the L shortcut (Psynergy menu, member row, L on
  the member, pick it) and press L facing the target.
- START opens the pause/save menu only when no message is up; a message left open swallows it. Close messages first.
- The field menu's icon bar opens on A only when nothing is in front.
- autowalk's WALK_TAP A taps open menus when there is nothing to talk to; use FIGHT only and add dialogue taps by hand.
- Diagonal stairs: Goma Cave's bridge room, DOWN+LEFT from 36,14 (not from 35,14).
- People standing on a 2-cell corridor block it (McCoy's red carpet): walk the other column (x 244 for column 15).
