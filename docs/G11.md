## 3. Routing notes (add to G9.md's tools)

- Slopes (shape kinds 1/2) push the leader sideways when he turns north/south on them: put waypoints on the flat cell
  past the slope (e.g. 14,27 not 15,27; 2,66 then 2,65; 5,89 then 5,88).
- A rock or NPC actor can block a cell the pathfinder thinks free (radius ~12 px): `tests/probes/actors.cjs` +
  `PATH_BLOCK`, or a 4-8 frame sideways nudge before a vertical leg (the exhausted strongman at 180,1311).
- autowalk only finishes when it reaches a waypoint or sticks with the field present: end a leg exactly on a monster
  cell, or the battle (no leader) leaves it unfinished and no keys are printed.
- Event loops: A on a talkable NPC restarts his talk; after the last message stop tapping A (the strongman, f31600).
- Scratch probes used this session (not committed, port_work/g11/probes): `hgrid.cjs` (one character per cell:
  height/4 or slope kind), `findbeh.cjs` (cells with a behaviour), `clip.sh` (autowalk keys up to a frame),
  `runs2wp.js` (pathfind runs -> autowalk waypoints).
