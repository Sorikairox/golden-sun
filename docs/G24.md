## 1. The transition check (GS23's open item): game behaviour

The Lower's exit 1 into the Entrance opens with the interlaced wipe. `MercuryLighthouse_Initialize` sets
`gFieldWork->transition = SCENE_TRANSITION(TRANSITION_WINDOW, WINDOW_TRANSITION_INTERLACED_WIPE)` for every scene of that
overlay (Lower, Middle, Upper, Exterior), so all their exits use it (the Exterior's waterfall arrival shows it too).
screen_transition.c's case: even lines' window 0 runs from x 0 to v, odd lines' from 240 - v to 240, with
v = 240 * (value & 31) / 32, inside all layers (0x7f7f), outside BG0 only (1), the two swapped by bit 5 of the progress.
Once v passes 120 the centre is drawn in full and both sides keep alternate lines, which is what GS23 saw.

Measured on our frames (port_work/g24/tools/wipe.cjs compares each line with the finished room): f7208 even line 40 full to
x 134, odd line 41 full from x 105 (v = 135 = 240 * 18 / 32, 240 - v = 105); f7212 even to 194, odd from ~48 (v = 195 =
240 * 26 / 32; the few px off are the water animation). Two steps of progress per frame. The side lines that are not full
show only BG0 (a darker, partial room), as the outside mask says. Port PPU (port/pc/ppu.c) reads WIN0H per line: correct.
