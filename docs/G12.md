## 4. Routing notes (add to G9/G11's)

- **A in the field opens the menu** when nothing is in front: tap **B** to close messages near the end of a cutscene,
  or stop A exactly after the last message (autowalk's WALK_TAP taps A, so it opens menus in free control).
- autowalk's default WALK_STUCK (90) ends a walk on any cutscene: raise it (WALK_STUCK=700+) for event cells, or
  stop the walk before the trigger and script the taps.
- Puppies/guards/villagers that "turn Isaac back" are story gates: read the scene's trigger table conditions before
  walking around them (FLAG_MET_SATUROS_AND_MENARDI for the puppies).
- Mapdump 'J' cells are jump cells: a step toward a J two cells away over a pit jumps. A small BFS over the dump
  (steps of 1, or 2 over a wall) found the stepping-stone chain (scratch `port_work/g12/stones.js`).
- Scratch helpers this session (not committed, port_work/g12): `run.sh` (base routes + extra keys + shots +
  sheet), `c.sh` (same from the day-one save), `walk.sh` (autowalk from the save), `probes/beh.cjs` (cells per
  behaviour), `probes/shape.cjs` (behaviour/kind/heights per cell).
