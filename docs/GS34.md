# GS34: full-screen menus showed borrowed tiles as the map in the zoomed margins (session GS34, 2026-11-05, bug fix)

Bug (seen by GS33, docs/GS33.md 5): at Map zoom Most (and More), on the field menu's Status screen over a field map, the
margins around the GBA's 240 x 160 showed wrong tiles: pink/orange ground, a "lava" pattern where Kolima Forest's river
runs at the left. Live build 6f385dc (GS1 site de3551885fbd8107).

On `main`: 8442a83 (margins), ae64e65 (parked sprites), 374ff7f (Djinn figures), then expected.tsv and the docs.
**Not deployed, not pushed.**

## 1. Root cause (evidence)

- The Status screen does not change any display register: DISPCNT 0x1f40, BG0-3CNT 0400 0709 060a 0503 on every frame
  from the field (f1990) through Status (f2765-f2860) (tests/boot.cjs --regs). So GS29's test "this BG is the map"
  (map layer = BG1-3 on screen block 5 + 3 - bg, text 256 x 256, BG map's first/last columns equal the grid's cells)
  stays true: the tilemaps (screen blocks 5-7) are untouched.
- What it changes is under them. `StatusScreen_Run` (vendor menu/menu/status.c) draws a 30 x 20 border (the whole GBA
  screen, uploaded at the next VBlank), waits a frame, then **saves and overwrites BG palettes 0-1** (the first sprite
  palette, for the element icons it draws as window tiles) and **8 KB of char block 1** (`STATUS_SAVED_TILE_BYTES`,
  its text canvas, filled with 0x33333333); BG3 (the ground, char base 0) takes its tiles 512+ from char block 1.
  It puts both back before the border goes. Measured: BG palette 0-1 CRC and char block 1 CRC change at f2763 and are
  restored at f2864, while the window layer covers the screen from f2763 to f2866.
- On the GBA nothing of the map shows meanwhile (the window layer is opaque in front, but for one transparent corner
  pixel per window corner: 12 pixels, where the GBA itself shows the borrowed palette). The margins, though, were drawn
  from the grid's cells with the **live** char data and palette, so they showed the borrowed bytes as the map; with
  Menus: Edges (GS33) the GBA-screen pixels that moved windows uncover showed the same.
- Same mechanism, less visible: the field **Djinn** and **Item** screens (yellow/garbled ground tiles in place of the
  river, digests zoom_menus_field f2400/f2600 before vs after) and the **World Map's** Status and Djinn screens
  (specks of palette 0-1 colours in the still picture's ground: GS30 draws it with the live palette).
- Not GS33's: the base build at Menus Centre shows it too (contact sheet columns "before").

## 2. Fix (renderer only, port/pc/ppu.c GS1 half; no game logic touched)

- **cover_check / cover_frame** (line 0 of a zoomed field frame or a zoomed World Map still-picture frame): from VRAM and
  line 0's registers, is the window layer (the BG on screen block 4) in front of every other BG, unblended, not
  windowed, and opaque on the GBA's 240 x 160 but for at most 64 pixels (the window corners)? That is what the frame
  really shows, so there is no frame of lag (the clobber comes a frame after the border is on screen; on close the
  palette is back before the border goes: measured above).
- Not covered: the BG palette (512 B) and BG VRAM (64 KB) are copied (zoomed frames only).
- Covered: every layer but the window layer is drawn **in the margins** from that copy (cells from the live grid, the
  camera and registers live, they do not move under these screens): the map as it was when the screen opened. The
  World Map still picture's margins take the copy's palette (sWmPal). The backdrop there too. The GBA's own 240 x 160
  stays the hardware's (window corners included) unless "Menus: Edges" lays the windows out, when the map under moved
  windows is the copy's. Without a copy of the same kind (none since the frame zoomed) those layers are left out and the
  backdrop is black.
- **Parked sprites** (ae64e65): menus park unused sprites off the GBA's screen (the Status screen's page arrows at
  x 256 / y 216 and x 248 / y 168; its Djinn figures at y 210 on the Psynergy/details pages); the zoomed OAM range drew
  them in the margins (swords at the frame's corners, Djinn above the help window, a stray sprite beside the World Map's
  Djinn screen). On a zoomed frame a sprite wholly off the GBA's 240 x 160 is skipped when it is a menu's (VBlank OAM
  tags) or when the frame is covered. Field actors in the margins of other frames are drawn as before.
- **Djinn figures follow their window** (374ff7f, Menus: Edges): the Status screen's four figures are field sprites at
  depth 255; GS33's party-member rule took depths 238-254, so at Most they stayed below the moved status window, over
  the map. Now 238-255 (still only inside a window).
- Normal: none of it runs (sCoverOn = sCoverBlack = 0, menu_parked only on LW != 240); Centre and Edges both fixed.

## 6. Notes

- Under a full-screen menu the margins are a still picture of the map (no tile/water animation), like GS30's World Map
  menus; actors under covered frames are drawn only where the GBA would draw them.
- A screen that borrows map VRAM without covering the GBA's whole screen would still show it in the margins (as the
  GBA shows it in its own view); none found.
- With Edges, the full-screen border drawn for a frame or two while the Status screen opens/closes (not a window in
  gWindowWork) stays at the GBA's place while the windows already sit at the edges (GS33's rule for window-layer
  content outside windows); transient, not changed.
