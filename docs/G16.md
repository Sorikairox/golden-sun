## 3. The cornering chase (how it works, for the route)

LogRoomsA_UpdateActorStops moves Ivan and the two suspects over 37 stops (gLogRoomsA_Stops: an outer rectangle, grid x
0..24 by z 0..8 in 8-pixel units at pixel x = 72 + 8 gx, z = 632 + 8 gz, plus two crossbars at gx 8 and 16). A stop is
occupied within 16 px of the leader, 8 px of Ivan or a suspect (current place or destination). Each suspect picks a random
turn at a stop, reverses when that stop is occupied, and is blocked when the reverse is occupied too; 30 frames of a block
raise event 201-203 (LogRoomsA_ReadCorneredSuspectsMinds). Ivan keeps walking his facing (he takes the leader's facing only
when the leader pushes into him). Standing still blocks Ivan; walking the leader round the loop against Ivan's direction
squeezes everything between them. The route's leg (UP the east crossbar, LEFT along the top row) cornered both at once.
