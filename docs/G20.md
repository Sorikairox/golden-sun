## 1. The Tret Tree's layout (from tree_rooms.c, confirmed by play)

the second floor (x 42-58); bottom row the third floor (x 2-18), the fourth floor (x 22-38), the fifth floor (x 42-58).
TretTreeBranch (scene 46) is the outside: branches are thin lines of cell heights with sky (height 0) between them; its exits
1-8 are on the trunk (x 20-26) and lead to TreeRooms entrances 2-9 (branch exit n -> TreeRooms entrance n + 1).

## 5. Checked, no port bug found

0 distinct stubs hit on every route.

- **Jagged vertical edges during the falls** (frames show half a room, a ragged edge and black): the falls' scene change uses
  `SCENE_TRANSITION(TRANSITION_WINDOW, WINDOW_TRANSITION_NOISE)` (tree_rooms.c), the same noise wipe GS14/GS15 identified.
  Game behaviour.
- **"Flint is set to Isaac!"** when Isaac's first battle command picks the Djinn list: Flint was still on standby since the
  GS19 unleash (unleashed Djinn wait on standby until set or summoned); setting it raised Isaac's max HP 106 -> 115 (class
  change). Game behaviour.
- **Garet's max HP rose 114 -> 125 while walking** after the Cures: a Djinni recovering from the GS19 summon is set again
  after some steps (class change). Game behaviour.
- The tree's rooms tint slowly (TretPalette_UpdateMapTint every 32 frames) and its first layer is scaled to 9/10 vertically
  (tree_rooms.c `layer->scaleY = 9 * Q16_ONE / 10`): both by design.
- The Tret Tree's random battles (Spiders, Drone Bees, Gnomes, Troll pairs with Brute Force for 43) wipe a Lv 9 party that
  only attacks; grinding there failed twice (a wipe: the party woke at Kolima's restart point, as GS19 found).

## 8. Routing notes

- Cocoons: the first landing tears one (its flag); landing again breaks through. Torn cocoons are jumped from like jump cells
  (the leader keeps going two cells per push over the web walls), so the webs are a jump puzzle; the falls give the order.
- Vines on the branches are step triggers: give autowalk the cell the trigger moves the leader to (40,20, 36,11, 8,12), not
  the trigger cell, or it walks back.
- Climbs (behavior 253) change the leader's height, not his cell: use WALK_STUCK=500.
- The tree's hole into Tret's glade: from the hole DOWN ~90 frames, LEFT ~30, DOWN (climbs to the ground at about 20,16).
