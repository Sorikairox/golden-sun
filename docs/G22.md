## 3. Checked, no port bug found

0 distinct stubs hit on every route. No asm/C change: no EQUIV spec, the wasm was not rebuilt.

- **The ice room was never boxed in** (G21 4): autowalk's vertical legs stop as soon as the leader enters a cell, so he walks the
  next leg along that cell's edge, where the pillars' collision (26,18 at z 296, 20,18 at z 288) blocks him. Ending a leg in the
  row below (row 20, then up to row 19) gives z 314 and the row is open. The behavior-10 cells at x 30-33 are not slopes but the
  torches' push triggers (STEP_TRIGGER_WALKING 10).
- **The torch puzzle is not needed to leave**: the corridor south of the torches (column 34) reaches exit 3 directly. The melt
  opens the jump across the puddle at 36,23 to the passage north-east of it (not followed this session). As in the vendor
  tables; not a bug.
- **Imil's weapon and armor shops are closed** until Saturos is beaten at Mercury Lighthouse (imil.c: the sellers lie in bed,
  Imil_TalkToWeaponMerchant pans to them, "store closed until well"). Only the item shop sells (Herb 10, Antidote 20, Elixir
  30, Sacred Feather 70): better gear is not on sale at this point of the story.
- **Megan's Yes answer gives "Oh. That's too bad. That's where Mia was headed."**: message +1 of MsgImilHaveVisitedOldCoupleWho
  is the Yes reply (en.po 05464); the No reply is "Mia went to their house." Game text.
- **Imil's chimney smoke is lavender**: Imil_SpawnAmbientEffects spawns SPRITE_DUST_PUFF with SPRITE_COLOR_BLUE_TINT ("blue
  raised to full", field/sprite.h): tan dust with full blue reads lavender. Game behaviour.
- **The screen turns grey, magenta, red or blue during field Psynergy** (Ply in the old couple's house, Move, Mia's Ply at the
  door): the cast tint is chosen by `gFrameCount & 7` (trap 42).
- **Mercury Lighthouse's dark purple margins** (the corridor and the pool room): BG2 (the map's middle layer) draws them; with
  BG2 off the margins are black (port_work/g22/layer.cjs, diagnosis only), DISPCNT is mode 0 as the field sets it
  (MapGrid: BG3/BG2/BG1), the backdrop is black. Map data from the ROM, not a renderer fault.
- **The Venus summon on the Lizard Man (33)**: 30 + 3 % of 129 = 33, Venus resistance 100 (element preset 33 in the ROM at
  gEnemy_ElementPresetTable + 33 * 24 = 0x08089150: Venus 85/100, Mercury 100/127, Mars 85/72, Jupiter 85/100) against Isaac's
  ~104: x1.02. Flint's unleash 31. In range. (G21 1's "gEnemy_ElementPresetTable + 8 * 24 (0x08088e38)" is a typo: 0x08088e38
  is the table's start; preset 8 is at 0x08088ef8.)
- **Mia's menus** (Psynergy list, HP/PP, class Water Seer, the Djinn screen with Fizz): as the game draws them; no Douse yet
  (the Douse Drop comes later in the lighthouse).
- **Continue puts the party at a scene entrance-like spot**, not always where it saved (bilibin_ice: 7,26; lizard_man: 8,10):
  the game's own restore. Not a bug.
