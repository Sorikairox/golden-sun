## 3. Menus suspend scene tasks (trap 41 fix) confirmed

Only the Djinn and Status screens, and the Item screen in the international editions (TBS_PATCH_1), call
Menu_SuspendFieldTasks (menu/menu/shared.c); the Start pause menu ("Save your quest / Sleep / Change settings") and the English
Psynergy screen (TBS_PATCH_2 only) do not. Checked at Sol Entrance after the eruption (mt_aleph route, f26300), where the scene
overlay adds SolSanctumEntrance_UpdateEruptionRumbles as a frame task:

- `tests/probes/tasks.cjs` (task table on change): before the menu `5:10142/c80`; with the field menu's Status screen open
  (f26443) `0:10142/d80` (priority high byte bit 0 set: suspended); after B closes it (f27002) `0:10142/c80` again. The
  main-image tasks (PaletteFade_Update, Actor_UpdateAllThumb, MapGrid_UpdateLayerScroll, ActorSystem_PlaceSprites,
  WindowSystem_*) stay at c8x/480. Index 0x10142 = `ov37f_SolSanctumEntrance_UpdateEruptionRumbles` (wasm element + name
  sections; scratch port_work/g15/wtab.cjs).
- On screen: under the Start pause menu the field keeps shaking (frames port_work/g15/mp_open/, `tests/probes/bgscroll.cjs`:
  541 scroll changes in f26600-29300 with the pause menu open, 541 with no menu: the same RNG-driven shakes, as the game
  intends); the Status screen covers the whole field (port_work/g15/mq_item/), so there the probe is the evidence.

## 4. The jagged black half-screen (GS14 route f23880-23900): the game's own wipe

sol_sanctum_statue.c:369 cuts to Kraden by the hole with `SCENE_TRANSITION(TRANSITION_WINDOW, 2)`, 16 frames; variant 2 is
WINDOW_TRANSITION_NOISE (field/field_sequence/screen_transition.c:223): per scanline a window from the left edge to
`16 * step + Random16() % 16` pixels, inside and outside swapped by bit 5 of the step (close, then open). Measured on our
frames (port_work/g15/wipe/, scratch edge.cjs): f23876 black from the left 16-31 px on every line (step 1), f23880 144-159 px
(step 9): exactly 16 x step plus 0-15 at random. Not a port bug; no change.

- A raised door platform (Sol Entrance) or a cliff (Vault) refuses DOWN/UP; look for steps at the sides. A door in a cliff
  face (Vault's jail at 4,10) fires when walking north into it.

  leader sideways).
