## 1. The level target and the way down

- **The lift is not reachable before the fight.** The aerie's triggers: behavior 6 calls the lift (12,9), 12 rides it (6,10),
  behavior 5 (20,14 and 21,14) starts the confrontation while FLAG_MERCURY_LIGHTHOUSE_AERIE_ALEX_APPEARED is clear. The way
  from the platforms (24,12) to the west half crosses row 14 at columns 20-21 (hpath: no path with those two cells blocked).
  So before Saturos the only way down is to lose: GS25's `ml_saturos_loss` route wakes the party in Imil's Sanctum (scene 51,
  42,16; leader at 1 HP). GS25's hand-off said the lift was usable before the fight: corrected here. Game behaviour.
- **Fireball does not shrink with levels**: it is Psynergy (power 65 x element multiplier x range fall-off), so a level only
  adds HP, Attack and the order of turns. At Lv 10 one Fireball takes ~225 of 433 party HP. Lv 15 gives 570 HP (158/178/104/130)
  and Attack ~+20 %; Mia's Wish needs the Cleric class (Mercury level 9: four Mercury Djinn, two exist), Ply Well comes at 16.
  The exp table (gCharacter_LevelExpTable) puts Lv 15 at ~10800-11800 for each member, ~8000 more than Lv 10: about 120 World
  Map battles near Imil (~90 exp each). Target: Lv 15. It was reached, and Saturos still needs good turn order (section 4).
- **The lighthouse's puzzle flags**: Scene_Arrive clears FLAG_SCENE_TEMP on every scene entry and FLAG_AREA_TEMP (pipes,
  stairs, fountain, the five statues, the gap statue) when the area changes (the World Map is another area, so the waterfall's
  rise clears them too). Kept for good: CHANNELS_OPEN, HEART_HONORED, STATUE_FELL, both GATE_HELD_OPEN, the door, Mia, the
  Lizard Man, Sleet. FLAG_MERCURY_LIGHTHOUSE_LIT (Mia's +4 PP a turn) is also cleared on every area change.
- **Gear**: Imil's weapon and armor sellers lie sick until FLAG_SATUROS_BEATEN_AT_MERCURY (imil.c); the item shop sells Herb,
  Antidote and Elixir only. No gear was bought.
