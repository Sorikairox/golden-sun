# G19 field notes (session GS19, 2026-11-02): Kolima Forest to the Tret Tree by real play

Real play only (no warp/party/encounter probes), from the GS18 `kolima_forest` save. Story order read from the vendor scene
sources first (kolima_forest_entrance.c, kolima_forest_glade.c, kolima_forest_cliff.c, kolima_forest_floodgate.c,
kolima_forest_tree.c, tree_rooms.c). Routes found with hpath (section 4) and walked with autowalk; battles by autowalk's A
taps, grindbot or battlebot.

talks to Laurel and Tret, climbs into the Tret Tree and beats the evil inside Tret (tree_rooms.c, FLAG_TRET_DEFEATED). The
curse is lifted only later, with Hermes' Water from Mercury Lighthouse's fountain (after Imil). So Kolima's restored
villagers, Granite in Kolima and McCoy's reward come after Mercury Lighthouse, not straight after this stretch.

## 3. Checked, no port bug found

0 distinct stubs hit on every route. Compared with the vendor sources: the logs' rolling and the river logs tipping in
(cell copies), the floodgate's water levels and its spray and line shake (frames show a few torn top lines while it shakes:
KolimaForestFloodgate_ShakeLines), the floating logs' jump cells, Tret's purple tint (background palettes only, sprites
untinted, as PALETTES_BG), Laurel's and the wrathful side's portraits, the cursed palettes (KolimaPalette_Curse: redder in
the glade and cliff, dark and grey-green where Tret stands).

- **Battle backgrounds** match the vendor's `graphics/battle_backgrounds/kolima_forest.png` (the logs, stumps and trees, in
  the forest's battles) and `tret_tree_interiors.png` (the wooden walls, cobweb, green vine and the yellow spiky floor).
- **A lost battle** (a grind try with Isaac out of PP, f65396) sends the party to Kolima's restart point (scene_rules.s
  `restart_point Kolima, -1, Kolima, 12`) with only the leader at 1 HP and the others down: the game's rule
  (field_sequence/run.c: "back to the last sanctum with 1 HP"; Kolima's healer is a tree while the curse lasts). G18's note
  that a loss woke the party at the Vault Sanctum "with full HP" is probably the healer's work there (not rechecked).
  Not a bug.
- **PP comes back by walking** (one PP every 160 px outside towns, walkingDistanceScale): grinding with Cure lasts only while
  battles are fought with PP in hand, hence grindbot's GR_FLEE.
- **Corner gaps between stumps are not passable**: the player control probes 8 px ahead at the heading and +-22.5 and +-45
  degrees (player_control.c), so two stumps touching at a corner block a diagonal; hpath's diagonals (HPATH_DIAG) overstate
  this and autowalk's 'd' waypoint confirmed the block at the entrance.

## 6. Routing notes

- Kolima Forest's stumps fill most of their cell (12 x 12 px at 32 px high): treat them as walls; HPATH_MAP=3 shows them.
- Logs roll until a cell does not fit (a wall, a stump, another log; on the floodgate's riverbed a cell that is not flat), so
  order matters: the cliff needs the river log rolled west, back east, the west-east log north, and the other log south to
  get out again.
- Push a log from its middle: from a cell edge the leader slides past its end (centre with WALK_CENTER=1, or walk into it).
- The floodgate's switch is cell (19,28) (behavior 9); A from 19,27 facing south. The sign one cell east answers A first if
  the leader faces it.
- Tret's vine: from 20,13 step LEFT ~14 frames onto 19,12, UP, then RIGHT ~28 frames, UP into the hole.
- autowalk FIGHT must be ~40 (frames between A taps); FIGHT=1 holds A, which stalls battles.
- A message left open swallows the next walk keys: count the A taps of a talk (Tret's: seven 60 frames apart).
