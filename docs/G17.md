## 3. Checked, no port bug found

Revive at a Sanctum (healing scene in grey, coins taken), the World Map walk and a Bat battle, the Goma Cave Entrance (waterfall,
pool posts, pillars pushed by walking and by Move, ladders, the stump leaping and sinking, Ivan's event with his portrait and
Whirlwind, the ivy blown off the cave mouth), the cave's rooms, stairs and jump cells, five cave battles (Djinn set, unleash, Ray,
the Venus summon's flash and damage, Earth Power message, level-up HP), the save screens. 0 distinct stubs hit on any route.

- **Field Psynergy tint colours differ per cast** (magenta, red, blue, grey for Move; blue and grey for Whirlwind): the game's
  own choice. FieldPsynergy_FadeToCastColor picks one of the eight gFieldEffect_TargetColors by `gFrameCount & 7` unless a
  FLAG_PSYNERGY_TINT flag forces one. Not a bug.
- **Move that only plays and ends** with the hand reaching empty air: no target found. Field_FindNearbyPsynergyTarget takes an
  actor only within 8 px of height of the leader (48 Catch, 64 Douse, 16 Mind Read) and, beyond 11 px, inside a cone of 3/32
  turn (about 34 degrees) either side of the facing rounded to a quarter turn (22.5 degrees beyond 19 px). The stump on its
  pedestal (y 64 vs the leader's 32) and the east pillar seen from 8 px lower in z (36 degrees off) both missed. The steering
  loop (FieldPsynergy_SteerPushedTarget) moves the target once per cast and ends on A/B/L/R.
- **Battle background** of the cave battles matches the vendor's `graphics/battle_backgrounds/goma_cave.png` (rock pillars,
  torches, the pool, the blue-green tiled floor), seen through the battle camera.

## 4. Routing notes

- Stairs drawn as diagonals (Goma Cave) take diagonal input: UP+LEFT / DOWN+RIGHT held. Straight presses stop at the foot.
- tests/probes/mapdump.cjs MAP_MODE=height prints shape byte 1 >> 4, but byte 1 is in 8-px units (trap 39), so y 32 prints 0.
  Scratch port_work/g17/hdump.cjs prints byte 1 raw (4 = y 32, 8 = y 64); its cave maps hold walls as heights, not behavior 255,
  so mapdump's '#' misses them. A height-aware BFS would make cave routing much faster (not written).
- Random encounters in Goma Cave come every ~200-400 steps; route probes without WALK_FIGHT end in a battle; with it, autowalk's
  A taps fight through.
- Move's target must be level with the leader (8 px) and close to his facing line; pull (press toward the leader) works when the
  leader is not on the cell the target moves to.
- Vault's Sanctum door: 3,17 from 3,19 going north (a villager stands on 5,18); its counter: 12,9 facing north (the woman wanders
  on 12,11).
